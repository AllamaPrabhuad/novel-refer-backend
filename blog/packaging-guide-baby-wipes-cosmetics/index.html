Choosing Packaging for Baby Wipes and Baby Cosmetics

Flip-top lids or sticker labels, pumps or flip-caps, tubes or jars — how to choose packaging for private label baby wipes, shampoos, lotions and creams.

Packaging does three jobs at once: it protects the product, it makes it easy for a tired parent to use, and it sells your brand. Here's how to choose formats for a baby care range.

Baby wipes

Flip-top lid or sticker label?

A rigid flip-top lid re-seals well and feels premium — common on 72–100 count packs. A re-sealable sticker label is lighter and lower cost, and works well for travel packs of 20–30 wipes.

Counts

Offer one family count (72 or 80 is common), one travel size, and a multipack for marketplaces.

Shampoos and washes

Pump bottles allow one-handed dispensing during bath time. Flip-cap bottles are lower cost and travel well. Add refill pouches once you have repeat buyers.

Lotions, creams and oils

  • Tubes are hygienic and portable — good for rash creams and face creams.
  • Jars suit rich creams and balms, and feel generous.
  • Flip-cap or pump bottles suit lotions and massage oils.

Practical checks before you commit

  1. Compatibility: the formula and pack must be stability-tested together.
  2. Line fit: make sure the bottle and closure run on the filling line.
  3. Label space: leave room for mandatory information, not just design.
  4. Shipping: check how packs travel in shipper cartons and marketplace fulfilment.

Start with stock components for speed and lower minimums, then invest in custom shapes once your range is proven.
