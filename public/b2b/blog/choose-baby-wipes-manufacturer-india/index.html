How to Choose a Baby Wipes Manufacturer in India: 12 Questions to Ask

The 12 questions to ask before choosing a private label baby wipes manufacturer in India — capacity, plants, substrates, lotion, microbiological quality, packaging, MOQ and more.

Baby wipes are one of the most frequently repurchased baby products — which makes them a great anchor for a brand, and makes your choice of manufacturer critical. A single bad batch, dried-out pack or stock-out can undo months of marketing. Use these questions to compare partners.

Capacity and supply

1. What is your monthly capacity — and how much is already committed?

Raw capacity tells you if a manufacturer can grow with you. Ask how many machines they run and what monthly output they produce. For reference, Novel Tissues runs nine wipes machines producing 1.5 crore+ wipes a month.

2. How many plants make wipes, and where?

A second plant means continuity if one line goes down, and regional plants cut freight time to your warehouses. Ask whether every plant runs the same specification and quality checks.

3. Can you scale my volumes without changing specifications?

Your wipe should feel the same at 10,000 packs a month as at 5 lakh.

Product and formulation

4. Which substrates and GSM options do you offer?

Expect spunlace in different blends and weights, and ideally plant-based options. See our guide to wipes GSM and substrates.

5. Do you formulate lotions in-house?

In-house formulation means faster customisation of water content, extracts and fragrance — and one party accountable for the result.

6. How do you control microbiological quality?

Wet wipes are mostly water, which makes preservation and hygiene critical. Ask about water treatment, preservative systems, preservative efficacy testing, environmental monitoring and batch release testing.

Packaging and operations

7. Which pack formats and lids can you run?

Flip-top lids, sticker labels, travel packs, multipacks — check that the formats your channels need are standard on their lines.

8. What drives your MOQ?

Usually printed flow-wrap film and lids. A good partner will explain the drivers and propose a practical first run.

9. What are your typical lead times for first and repeat orders?

First orders include artwork, film printing and approvals; repeat orders should be much faster. Agree a forecasting process.

Quality and partnership

10. What documentation comes with each batch?

Batch codes, release records and certificates of analysis protect you when a retailer or marketplace asks questions.

11. How do you protect my brand?

NDAs, restricted artwork access and confidentiality of your formula and pricing should be standard.

12. Can you make the rest of my range too?

If you plan to add shampoo, lotion or laundry detergent later, a partner that makes those too simplifies everything — one contract, one quality standard, one dispatch.

Next step

Shortlist two or three manufacturers, request samples and visit the plant if you can. The answers above — and how openly they're given — will tell you who to trust with your brand.
