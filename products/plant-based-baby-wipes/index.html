Choosing a plant-based substrate

Viscose is a cellulose fibre made from wood pulp; 100% viscose wipes are soft and absorbent and contain no synthetic polymer fibres. Bamboo-derived viscose and other plant-based fibres support natural positioning, but availability and cost vary — sample early. Our guide to wipes fabric explains the trade-offs.

Getting the claims right

Sustainability claims attract scrutiny from parents and regulators alike. We'll tell you exactly what your chosen substrate and pack can support, so your messaging is both persuasive and defensible.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Are 100% viscose wipes plastic-free?

The substrate contains no synthetic polymer fibres, but the whole product and pack determine what you can claim. We review claims with you before artwork is final.

Do you make bamboo wipes?

Bamboo-derived fibres are available on request, subject to supply. Ask for samples.

Are plant-based wipes softer?

100% viscose wipes are typically very soft and absorbent. Compare samples in hand.
