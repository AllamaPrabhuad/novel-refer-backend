What institutions buy

Maternity wards and neonatal units typically use large volumes of baby wipes, plus gentle washes and creams. New-mother kits add travel packs of wipes, a small shampoo or wash, lotion and rash cream. We manufacture all of these in your branding.

A note on medical products

Our baby wipes and liquids are personal care products. If you need sterile wipes or products with medical claims, tell us at the start — those follow different rules, and we'll advise what is and isn't possible.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you supply hospitals directly?

We supply hospital groups, maternity homes and the distributors and kit companies that serve them.

Can you make sterile wipes?

Our standard wipes are personal care products, not sterile medical devices. Tell us your requirement and we'll advise.

Can you make products for new-mother kits?

Yes — travel packs of wipes and small sizes of wash, lotion, oil and rash cream.
