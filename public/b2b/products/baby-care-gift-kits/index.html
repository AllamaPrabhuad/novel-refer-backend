Popular kit combinations

  • Bath time: shampoo or head-to-toe wash + lotion + wipes
  • Newborn essentials: water wipes + gentle wash + massage oil + rash cream
  • Travel kit: pocket wipes + travel-size wash and lotion
  • Laundry starter: baby detergent + fabric conditioner + bottle cleanser

Why one manufacturer matters for kits

A kit is only as good as its weakest product. Making every item with one partner keeps colours, fragrance families and quality consistent — and means one schedule instead of four.
