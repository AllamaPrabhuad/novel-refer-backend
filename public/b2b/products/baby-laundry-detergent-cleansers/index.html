FAQ

Laundry & Cleaners questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Ask our team.

Why do babies need a different laundry detergent?

Baby skin is thinner and more easily irritated, and clothes sit against it all day. Baby detergents typically use milder surfactants, rinse out cleanly and are often dye-free and fragrance-free.

Can you make low-foam detergent for front-load machines?

Yes, we offer low-foam variants for front-load washing machines.

Can the detergent be fragrance-free?

Yes. Many brands offer a fragrance-free variant alongside a lightly scented one.

How should bottle cleansers be used?

They're designed to rinse away cleanly. Directions on your label should tell parents to rinse thoroughly with clean, safe water after washing.

Can I order detergent with my wipes and cosmetics?

Yes — that's the point of working with us. One contract, one quality standard and combined dispatch.
