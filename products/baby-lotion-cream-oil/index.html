FAQ

Lotion, Cream & Oil questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Ask our team.

Can you make a zinc oxide diaper rash cream?

Yes, as a cosmetic barrier cream. Products making medicinal or treatment claims can fall under drug rules instead, so we review the positioning and claims with you before formulation is final.

Do you make Ayurvedic or herbal baby oils?

We work with botanical oils and extracts. Positioning a product as Ayurvedic falls under separate AYUSH regulations, so tell us early if that's your plan.

Can lotions be fragrance-free?

Yes. Many baby brands offer a fragrance-free variant for newborns and sensitive skin.

What is the MOQ for private label baby lotion?

It depends on batch size and packaging. Tell us your volumes and we'll suggest the smallest practical first batch.

How do you decide shelf life?

Through stability and compatibility testing of your final formula in your final pack.
