Privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

This policy explains how Novel Tissues ("we", "us") handles personal data collected through babywipesmanufacturer.com. We collect only what we need to respond to business enquiries, and we process it in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Details you give us in our forms: your name, company, work email, phone or WhatsApp number, business type, the products you're interested in, volumes, notes, and — for sample requests — a delivery address; for visit requests, your preferred date and time.
  • Technical data: standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) used to keep the site secure and working.
  • Analytics: when enabled, Google Analytics collects anonymised information about how visitors use the site, such as pages viewed.
  • Your browser: the site stores your form details in your own browser so you don't have to type them twice. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, send the brochure, dispatch samples and arrange visits or calls you request.
  • To follow up about private label manufacturing you have asked us about.
  • To understand which pages are useful and improve the site.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run the site and respond to you — such as our hosting provider, email provider, spreadsheet tools used by our sales team, and analytics when enabled — and only as needed for those purposes. We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you and manage any business relationship that follows, and then delete or anonymise them unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw your consent, or raise a grievance. Write to sales@noveltissues.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and enquiry data is stored on our server outside the public website folder, with access limited to our team.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this policy: sales@noveltissues.com.
