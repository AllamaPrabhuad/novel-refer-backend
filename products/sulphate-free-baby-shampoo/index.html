Sulphate-free vs tear-free

They are different claims. Sulphate-free means the formula contains no sulphate surfactants such as SLS or SLES. Tear-free depends on the whole surfactant system, the pH and testing of the final formula. Our tear-free formulation guide explains the details.

Yes, though usually a little less than sulphate-based formulas. We tune the system so it still feels satisfying in use.

Can you add my fragrance?
