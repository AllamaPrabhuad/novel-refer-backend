FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you make baby wipes in North India?

Yes. One of our two wipes plants is in North India, and both plants run the same specifications and quality checks.

Can you also supply shampoo and lotion to the north?

Yes. Our liquids are made at our Pondicherry campus and shipped to your warehouses alongside your wipes plan.

What if one plant is busy?

With wipes plants in both the north and the south, we can balance production between them.
