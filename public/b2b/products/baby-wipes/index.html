FAQ

Baby Wipes questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Ask our team.

What GSM is best for baby wipes?

Most everyday baby wipes sit in the middle of the 40–60 GSM range. Lighter fabrics keep costs down; heavier fabrics feel thicker and more premium. We'll sample two or three weights so you can compare them in hand.

Can you make plant-based or plastic-free wipes?

We offer plant-based substrates such as 100% viscose. Any 'plastic-free' or 'biodegradable' claim must match the exact substrate and its test data, so we confirm the claim before it goes on pack.

What is the MOQ for private label baby wipes?

It depends mainly on printed packaging — flow-wrap film is printed in rolls. Share your expected monthly volume and we'll propose the smallest practical first run.

How do you keep wipes fresh until the last sheet?

Lotion formulation, preservation, seal integrity and lid choice all matter. Each variant's shelf life is established through stability testing on the final pack.

Can you supply both North and South India?

Yes. With plants in both regions, we can dispatch from whichever is closest to your distribution hubs.

Do you make baby wipes for export?

Yes. Tell us your destination market early so formulation, labelling and documentation are planned for it.
