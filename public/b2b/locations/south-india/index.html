What we make for South Indian brands

Baby wet wipes in every count from pocket packs to 100+; tear-free shampoos and washes; lotions, creams, massage oils and rash creams; and baby laundry detergents and cleansers. Every product can carry your brand, your formula choices and your pack design.

Why make in the south

Making close to where you sell shortens freight time and lowers the cost of replenishment. With wipes and liquids both made in the region, you can plan fewer, fuller shipments to your hubs in Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Kochi or Coimbatore.

Tell us where your warehouses are and we'll plan dispatch from the plant that gives you the best freight and replenishment time.
