Questions buyers ask

Everything you'd ask on a first call.

Still curious? Send us your question and our private label team will reply.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQs depend on the product, pack format and how much of the packaging is custom. Share your expected volumes and we'll propose the lowest viable first run — and a path to scale from there.

Can I launch wipes and liquids together?

Yes — that's what makes us different. Wipes are made at our North and South India plants and all liquids at our Pondicherry campus, under one contract and one quality standard, so your full range launches together.

Can you use my own formula?

Absolutely. We can manufacture to your formula under NDA, or you can start from our baby-safe bases and customise fragrance, actives, texture and claims.

Do you help with packaging and artwork?

We guide you on pack formats, sizes and components, and check your artwork against label requirements before print. Request a sample kit to see our standard pack formats in hand.

Which wipes formats can you make?

Baby wet wipes in counts from travel packs of 10 up to 100+, with a choice of substrate, fabric weight (GSM), lotion and lid. Hand & face wipes and fragrance-free sensitive variants are available too.

What baby liquids do you manufacture?

Baby shampoo, head-to-toe wash, body wash, bubble bath, lotion, cream, massage oil, hair oil, rash cream, baby laundry detergent, fabric conditioner, bottle & nipple cleanser and toy & surface cleanser — across eight lines in Pondicherry.

Can I get samples before committing?

Yes. Request a sample kit from this site and we'll share the relevant wipes and bases so you can feel the difference before you finalise specs.

Do you supply for export?

Yes. Tell us your destination market and we'll discuss the formulation, labelling and documentation it needs.
