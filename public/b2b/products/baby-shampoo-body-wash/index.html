FAQ

Shampoo & Wash questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Ask our team.

What makes a baby shampoo tear-free?

Mainly the choice of very mild surfactants and a near-neutral pH, plus testing to support the claim. Our guide to tear-free formulation explains it in detail.

Can you make sulphate-free baby shampoo?

Yes. We offer sulphate-free cleansing systems built on mild amphoteric and non-ionic surfactants.

Can I use my own fragrance?

Yes. We can work with your fragrance house's oil, or suggest light baby fragrances. For newborn positioning, many brands also offer a fragrance-free variant.

What is the MOQ for private label baby shampoo?

It depends on batch size, pack size and whether components are stock or custom. Share your volumes and we'll recommend the smallest practical first batch.

Can you fill my own bottles?

Often, yes — subject to compatibility trials on our filling lines. We can also recommend standard bottles and closures.

How is shelf life decided?

Through stability and compatibility testing of your final formula in your final pack.
